## 1. The event

- **Name:** Philippine Startup Challenge XI (PSC XI) — 11th edition; PSC has run since 2014 [S2]
- **Organizer:** Department of Information and Communications Technology (DICT) — ICT Industry
  Development Bureau (IIDB) [S2, S3]
- **Region / round entered:** National Capital Region (NCR) [S1]
- **Stage targeted:** **Ideation** for the application. The team plans to move to the
  Prototyping stage (MVP) only after the FMD seed/work/docs are settled [founder, 2026-10-07].
  PSC XI accepts entries at the **Ideation** stage (a clearly defined business idea) or the
  **Prototyping** stage (a proof of concept or prototype). A working product is not required to
  apply [S2].
- **Track / category:** No separate tracks. Entries must be ICT-enabled and leverage one or more
  of: Software as a Service (SaaS), Internet of Things (IoT), Artificial Intelligence, Web3,
  Blockchain, Mixed Reality, or other ICT-related products or services [S2].
- **Official page / rules link:** https://iidb2026.my.canva.site/psc-xi-website [S2]
- **Application guidelines:** referenced on the official site ("check the application
  guidelines") but not yet read — `unknown — founder to share or link the guidelines document`
- **NCR application form:** Google Form linked from [S1]/[S2] (the URL pasted in [S1] was
  truncated; use the NCR link on the official site)
- **Inquiries:** psc_inquiries@dict.gov.ph [S2]

**Competition structure [S2]:**
1. Application → completeness and correctness check → assessment of the innovativeness of the
   proposed startup.
2. Up to **20 entries per region** are selected for the **mentorship phase** and the
   **Regional Competition**.
3. One **Regional Winner** per region (18 nationwide) advances to a **cluster-wide competition**
   (Luzon, Visayas, Mindanao). NCR competes in the **Luzon** cluster.
4. **9 teams go to the National Finals in Quezon City** — 4 from Luzon, 2 from Visayas,
   3 from Mindanao.
5. Finalists and winners may be connected with partner incubators after the program.

## 7. Non-negotiable event-specific claims

- The solution must be **ICT-enabled**, using SaaS, IoT, AI, Web3, blockchain, mixed reality, or
  another ICT-related product or service [S1, S2].
- The program is framed around **startup-readiness**: moving from ideation to a "stronger, more
  viable solution" with market validation, business model, and value proposition [S2]. A
  technically impressive build without a validated problem and business model works against
  the stated goals.
- The call emphasizes solving **real-world challenges** and creating **meaningful impact** [S1].
- The deck must name a **Sustainable Development Goal (SDG) priority** [S5] — the UN's 17 SDGs.
  The problem should map to an SDG genuinely; the SDG is chosen to fit the problem, not the
  other way round.
- The deck must include a **proposed use of funds / asks from investors** and **progress /
  traction** [S5]. At the Ideation stage with a ₱0 budget, these must be stated honestly (e.g.,
  validation interviews done, what funding would unlock) — never invented users or revenue
  (`INV-AS-004`).
